Privacy Policy

Knotting is a personal time tracker designed to keep your daily records private and under your control.

Effective: 25 September 2026 Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Information you create

Knotting processes the information you enter or generate while using the app, including:

  • Time entries and session start and end times
  • Activity titles, categories and notes
  • Daily titles and planned-activity status
  • App preferences and settings

This information is used only to provide Knotting's time-tracking, planning, synchronization and review features.

2. Local storage

Your Knotting information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to enable iCloud synchronization. Knotting does not require a separate account for its core features.

3. Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable iCloud synchronization, your Knotting information may be stored in your private iCloud database and synchronized between compatible Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

Apple processes this information under its own privacy policy and iCloud terms. Knotting's developer does not receive your iCloud password or Apple Account credentials. You can use Knotting without enabling this feature.

4. Optional Apple Calendar integration

Calendar integration is optional. Knotting requests access only after you choose to connect Apple Calendar, and you can choose which available calendars appear in Knotting.

For selected calendars, Knotting reads only the information needed to display events as read-only plans:

  • Event title
  • Start and end date and time
  • Calendar name or identifier
  • Calendar colour or other basic display information

Knotting does not intentionally copy calendar notes, locations, URLs, attendee information or other unnecessary event details into its storage.

Knotting never changes or deletes your original Apple Calendar events. Actions taken in Knotting apply only to Knotting's own records.

You can withdraw Calendar access at any time through Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars. Knotting will continue to work without Calendar access.

5. Analytics, advertising and crash reporting

Knotting does not use third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not sell your personal information.

Apple may provide aggregated or diagnostic information if you choose to share analytics and diagnostics with app developers in your device settings. This is handled through Apple's services.

6. Sharing

Knotting does not sell or rent your information or use it for advertising. Information is shared only as necessary to provide optional Apple services you enable, such as iCloud, CloudKit and Calendar, or where disclosure is required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it in Knotting, remove the app and its local data, or erase the device.

If iCloud synchronization is enabled, synchronized information may remain in your private iCloud database or on other connected devices. Deleting Knotting from one device may not remove data stored in iCloud or on your other devices. You can manage this data through your Apple Account and iCloud storage settings.

For help deleting Knotting information, email YufeiMeng_23@outlook.com.

8. Security

Knotting relies on Apple platform security and, when enabled, iCloud and CloudKit security. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and Apple Account with a passcode, a strong password and two-factor authentication.

9. Children's privacy

Knotting is not designed to collect children's personal information for advertising or profiling. Contact the developer if you believe information belonging to a child has been processed improperly.

10. International processing

When you enable services such as iCloud or CloudKit, information may be processed in locations determined by Apple under Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Knotting's features or privacy practices change. The revised policy will show an updated date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate method, such as an in-app notice or an updated App Store listing.
