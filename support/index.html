Frequently asked questions

How do I start tracking an activity?

Open Today and tap Start, choose an activity, optionally enter a title, and begin the timer. When multitasking, use the add-activity control to start another activity alongside the current one.

How do I stop one activity while multitasking?

Tap the live activity on the timeline to open its details, then stop that activity. Use Stop all only when you want to finish every active timer.

How do I edit or delete an entry?

Tap its block on the daily timeline. From the entry editor, you can update its activity, title, note, start time or end time, or delete the entry.

Does Knotting require an account?

No. Core tracking works without a separate Knotting account. Optional iCloud synchronization uses the Apple Account already signed in on your device.

Does Knotting change my Apple Calendar?

No. Calendar events are displayed as read-only plans. Starting or completing one in Knotting creates or updates only Knotting's own record; the original calendar event is never changed or deleted.

Apple Calendar help

How do I connect Apple Calendar?

In Knotting, open the add menu, select Apple Calendar, review the explanation and choose Connect. After granting access, choose the calendars you want to show.

I denied Calendar access. How do I enable it?
  1. Open the Settings app on your device.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security → Calendars.
  3. Select Knotting and allow the requested access.
  4. Return to Knotting and reconnect or refresh your calendars.
Why is a calendar missing?

Confirm that the calendar is enabled in Apple Calendar and included in Knotting's selected-calendar list. If it still does not appear, verify Calendar permission in Settings and restart Knotting.

iCloud synchronization help

How do I enable iCloud synchronization?

Make sure your device is signed in to iCloud, iCloud Drive is enabled, and Knotting is allowed to use iCloud. Then enable synchronization from Knotting's Sync settings.

Why is data not appearing on another device?
  • Confirm both devices use the same Apple Account.
  • Confirm iCloud Drive and Knotting's iCloud access are enabled on both devices.
  • Connect both devices to the internet and open Knotting.
  • Allow a few minutes for the first synchronization.
  • Check that your iCloud storage is not full.
Can I use Knotting without iCloud?

Yes. iCloud synchronization is optional. Without it, your Knotting records remain stored locally on the device where you created them.

Data and privacy

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual entries and activities within Knotting. If iCloud synchronization is enabled, deletions may synchronize to your other devices. Removing the app from one device may not remove data stored in your private iCloud database or on other devices.

How can I request deletion assistance?

Email YufeiMeng_23@outlook.com with the subject “Knotting Data Deletion.” Because Knotting does not operate a separate user-account database, support may guide you through removing local or private iCloud data on your devices.

For complete information about data handling, read the Knotting Privacy Policy.
